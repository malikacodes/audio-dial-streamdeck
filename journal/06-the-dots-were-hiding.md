# 06 · The dots were hiding

2026-10-12

A small one. The two dots on the strip tell me which of my two devices
has the sound. On a dial by itself they were fine. In a dial stack they
were gone.

## Where they went

Stream Deck draws its own little stack icon in the top right corner of
the strip, on top of whatever the plugin drew. My dots lived in the top
right corner. So the one thing that says "you're on the headphones" was
sitting under an icon that says "this is a stack", which I already knew,
because I made the stack.

The plugin can't tell whether it's in a stack, so there's no "move them
only when stacked". They just moved.

## Where they are now

Level with the percent, on the right, just above the volume bar:

```
USB Audio Device          (stack icon lives up here)
100%                 ● ●
▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬
```

I also slid them 3 pixels left. Up by the name nobody could tell they
hung past the end of the bar. Right above the bar it would have looked
crooked.

## What I learned

The corner of the strip isn't all mine. I designed the strip on a dial
by itself and then used it in a stack every day. Test it where it
actually lives.
